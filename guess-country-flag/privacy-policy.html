Guess the Country Flag: Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Guess the Country Flag (the “App”), published by softdeveloper, handles information. The App package is com.softdeveloper.guesscountryflag.

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to shkh_shehbaz@yahoo.co.in. Developer website: https://shaikhshehbaz.com.

Information stored on your device

The App stores gameplay and preference information locally on your device so it can provide its features and preserve progress. This can include level and puzzle progress, coins and rewards, achievements, daily-reward and streak state, hint or booster state, sound and vibration preferences, daily-reminder preference, and other settings needed for gameplay.

This local gameplay information is not a user account and is not uploaded to a server by the App. It remains on the device until you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App, subject to Android and Google Play behavior.

Firebase Analytics

The App uses Firebase Analytics to measure app usage and reliability in a privacy-conscious way. The App sends allow-listed gameplay, screen, advertising, purchase, and lifecycle events. Firebase may process information such as app instance identifiers, device and app information, approximate location inferred from an IP address, and interaction or diagnostic information. We do not use Firebase Analytics to ask for your name, contacts, photos, precise location, or account credentials.

Analytics collection is controlled by the App’s consent and telemetry settings where applicable. Firebase processes information under Google’s policies and terms.

Firebase Crashlytics

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify crashes, hangs, and serious errors so we can improve stability. Crashlytics may process crash reports, stack traces, app version, device model, operating-system information, and other technical diagnostics. The App’s crash reports are configured to use controlled categories and do not intentionally include names, contacts, passwords, payment details, or precise location.

Google Mobile Ads, AdMob, and UMP

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. It also uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain and manage consent choices where required by law.

Google may process information needed to request, display, measure, secure, and prevent fraud in advertising, including device and app information, IP address and approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and advertising or app-set identifiers made available by Android or Google Play. The App does not request precise location permission.

The App configures its Google Mobile Ads requests for child-directed treatment, under-age-of-consent treatment, and a maximum content rating of G. These settings are intended to disable interest-based advertising and remarketing; contextual, limited, measurement, security, and fraud-prevention processing may still occur. Consent choices, where shown, can be reviewed through the App’s privacy controls or Android/Google controls.

Google’s processing is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Google Play Billing

The App offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing, such as coin or feature packages. Google Play handles payment credentials and payment processing. The App receives purchase and entitlement information needed to grant or restore the purchased item; the App does not receive your full payment-card details.

Daily reminders and notifications

If you enable Daily Play Reminder, the App requests Android notification permission where required and schedules local reminders on your device. The reminders are scheduled locally and do not require a user account or a push-notification server. You can disable reminders in the App’s Settings or Android notification settings. Android may retain notification scheduling and delivery records according to its own operation.

Children’s privacy

The App is a family-friendly educational game and does not knowingly ask users to provide names, email addresses, contacts, photos, precise location, or other direct identifiers inside the game. The App does not require an account. Advertising requests use child-directed and under-age-of-consent treatment as described above. Parents and guardians can contact us at shkh_shehbaz@yahoo.co.in with questions or requests.

Data security and retention

Information sent by Google SDKs is transmitted using the security measures provided by those services. Local gameplay data is retained on your device until you clear it or uninstall the App. Firebase and Google retain information processed through their services according to their own policies, settings, and legal obligations. We do not control their independent retention periods.

Your choices

  • Use Android settings to clear the App’s local data or uninstall the App.
  • Change notification permission and daily-reminder settings.
  • Review available Google privacy and consent choices in the App and on your device.
  • Choose whether to view optional rewarded advertisements or make optional purchases.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App, its services, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date identifies the latest version.
